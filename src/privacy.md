# Privacy Policy — Trap & Run

**Last updated: 2026-10-25**

Trap & Run ("the app") is an independent two-player strategy board game for
Android and iOS; "we" and "us" means its developer. This policy explains what
the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. It is written to
be read, not skimmed; the short version is in the first section.

Contact: **[support email]**

## 1. The short version

- Playing **offline** (against the bot, or Pass & Play on one phone) sends
  nothing to us. Your settings and statistics stay on your device.
- Playing **online** creates a game account. We then store what the game
  needs to work: your display name, country flag, chosen picture and pet,
  your games and results, rating, friends and coins.
- We **do not** collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or
  device identifiers, and the app contains **no ads, no analytics SDK and
  no in-app purchases**.
- We **do not sell or share** your data with anyone. It is stored for us by
  Supabase, our hosting provider.
- You can **delete your account in the app** at any time (Profile → Delete
  account), or by emailing us.
- The app is for players aged **13 and over**.

## 2. What we store, and why

### 2.1 Offline play (nothing leaves your device)

The bot, Pass & Play, the tutorial and your settings work without an
account and without an internet connection. The app keeps the following on
your device only: sound, haptics and timer preferences; your chosen name,
picture, pet and country; bot difficulty preferences; local win/loss
statistics; an interrupted local game so you can resume it; and your coin
balance and unlocked items. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

### 2.2 Online play (a game account)

When you first use Play Online, Challenge a Friend or the leaderboard, the
app creates an account on our server. You may start as a **guest** (an
anonymous account tied to your installation) or **sign in with Google Play
Games** (Android) or **Game Center** (iOS) so that your progress follows you
to another phone.

For that account we store:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| An account ID we generate | To identify your account |
| Your Google Play Games player ID or Game Center player ID, if you sign in | To recognise you when you sign in again, and to stop anyone else from pretending to be you |
| Your display name | Shown to other players; must follow the name rules in the Terms |
| Your chosen country (shown as a flag), if you choose one | Shown on your profile, in games, to friends and on the country leaderboard. We never read your actual location; the app only suggests the country from your phone's region setting, and you can choose "Don't show" |
| Your chosen profile picture and pet | Shown to other players |
| Your online games: the moves, walls, timers, reactions and quick messages sent, and the result | To run the game between two phones, to check that every move is legal, and to settle who won |
| Wins, losses, your rating, its uncertainty and a history of rating changes per game | For ranked play, your profile and the leaderboards |
| Your friend code, friend list, friend requests, blocked players and game invites | For the friends and invite features |
| Whether you are currently in the app | So friends can see you are online and invite you |
| Your coin balance, coin transactions and unlocked pictures and pets | So coins and unlocks survive reinstalling or changing phones |
| Timestamps of the above | Normal database operation |

Reactions and quick messages are chosen from a fixed list; there is no free
text chat, and we never store anything you type other than your display
name and codes you enter.

### 2.3 Technical data

Like any internet service, our hosting provider's servers see your IP
address and basic request details when the app talks to them, and keep
standard server logs for a short time for security and troubleshooting. We
do not use this data to identify or profile you, and the app sends no crash
reports or usage analytics to us or to anyone else.

## 3. Who can see what

- **Other players** see your display name, picture, pet, country flag,
  rating, win/loss record and leaderboard position. Opponents also see your
  moves and reactions during a game.
- **Your friends** additionally see whether you are online.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third
  parties for their own purposes, and there are no advertising or analytics
  partners.
- **Service providers.** Our backend (database, authentication and realtime
  connections) runs on **Supabase**, which processes data on our behalf and
  under our instructions. Sign-in uses **Google Play Games Services** on
  Android and **Game Center** on iOS; when you sign in, Google's or Apple's
  own privacy policy applies to what those services do.
- **Legal requests.** We would disclose data only if required by law.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Your account data is kept while your account exists.
- When you delete your account, your profile, identity link, friendships,
  blocks, invites, coins and unlocks are deleted at once. A game in
  progress is counted as a loss for you. Finished games are kept so that
  your opponents' records and rating history stay correct, but your seat in
  them is emptied: no name, ID or picture of yours remains attached.
- Server logs at our hosting provider are kept for a short, fixed period and
  then discarded.

## 5. Deleting your account and your rights

**In the app:** Profile → Delete account. This works immediately and signs
the device out.

**Without the app:** email **[support email]** from the address linked to
your Google or Apple account, or include your friend code, and we will
delete the account within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, Brazil or
California), you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to
correct it, to object to or restrict its processing, or to complain to your
data-protection authority. Email us and we will help. We do not
discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.

**Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK).** We process account data because it is
necessary to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract),
and we check sign-in proofs and enforce the rules because we have a
legitimate interest in keeping the game fair and secure. Choosing a country
flag is optional and based on your consent, which you withdraw by choosing
"Don't show".

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal data from them. Online play, friends and display names are
the reasons for the 13+ rating. If you believe a child under 13 has created
an account, email us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
Game results, ratings and coins are computed on the server, and the app
cannot change another player's data. Sign-in proofs from Google and Apple
are verified on the server before an account is linked.

## 8. International transfers

Our servers are hosted by Supabase in **Singapore** (AWS ap-southeast-1).
If you play from another country, your data is transferred there to run the
game. Supabase provides standard contractual safeguards for such transfers.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
Check it now and then; material changes will be summarised at the top of
the page for at least 30 days.
