# Delete your Trap & Run account

You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time. There
are two ways.

## In the app (immediate)

1. Open Trap & Run and tap your picture on the Home screen to open **Profile**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. The account is deleted at once and the app signs you out.

## By email

If you no longer have the app, email **[support email]** with the subject
"Delete my account". Include the friend code shown on your Friends screen, or
the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, so we
can find the right account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

## What gets deleted

Your profile (name, picture, pet, country flag), your account and sign-in
link, your rating and rating history, your friends, friend requests and
blocks, pending invites, your coins and unlocked items, and any game in
progress (which counts as a loss). Finished games are kept so that your
opponents' records stay correct, but your seat in them is emptied: no name,
ID or picture of yours remains attached.

Settings and statistics for offline play live only on your phone and are
removed when you uninstall the app.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for the full picture.
