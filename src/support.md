# Support

Email **[support email]**. We read everything and usually reply within two
days. Include your phone model and Android version for anything technical.

## Quick answers

**How do I play?** Each player has a pawn and ten walls on a 9×9 board. On
your turn, move your pawn one square or place a wall to lengthen your rival's
route. Walls may slow a player but never shut them in: a path must always stay
open. The first pawn to reach the far side wins. The in-app tutorial
(Settings → How to play) walks you through every rule on a real board.

**Why can't I place this wall?** Either it overlaps or crosses another wall,
or it would leave one of the players with no path to their goal. The board
tells you which.

**What is the rating?** Ranked online games move a skill rating up or down
depending on the result and the opponent's strength. New players show a
question mark until the rating settles after a few games. The leaderboard
lists established players worldwide and per country.

**How do coins work?** Daily rewards and wins add coins; some losses and
leaving a game early remove a few, never below zero. Coins unlock profile
pictures and pets. Coins cannot be bought and have no money value. There are
no ads and no purchases in the game.

**I lost a game because my connection dropped.** Online games have a turn
timer, and a turn that runs out counts as a loss, the same as it would for your
opponent. If this keeps happening on a stable connection, email us with the
approximate time of the game.

**Someone is bothering me.** Open their profile from the game or your friends
list and tap **Block**. They can no longer find, add or invite you. Reactions
and messages are limited to a fixed set, so there is no free text to report,
but do tell us about names that break the rules.

**How do I delete my account?** See [Delete your account](delete-account.html).
